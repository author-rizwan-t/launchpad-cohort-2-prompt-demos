## Prompt

You are an expert storyteller and lyricist. I need you write a song about the world war. Remember these points while writing the song:

1. The song should contain the whole story about how the world war started
2. Take inspiration of your storytelling from the renowned writer J R R Tolkien.
3. The story should have some elements of epic fantasy containing powerful creatures, evil and resilient human
4. The storyline should send a message of peace at the end.

Use graduate level English for the song. 

## LLM Response

**Title: The Lament of War and the Dawn of Peace**

*Verse 1*  
In ancient days beneath a twilight sky,  
Discord stirred in realms both low and high;  
Whispers from shadowed halls of forgotten lore  
Foretold of strife that would shake the core.  
A spark of mortal pride and dread did ignite  
The flames of war in the deep of night.

*Chorus*  
O, sing the lament of war and fire,  
Of men and beasts in fierce, dark desire;  
Yet through the ashes, hope’s soft light is cast—  
For even shattered hearts may heal at last.

*Verse 2*  
A relic cursed from an age long past  
Awoke dark power, its spell was vast;  
From its eerie glow rose the call to fight,  
To rend all bonds of peace in endless night.  
Dragons of flame took to the stormy air,  
While valiant knights prepared with noble care.

*Verse 3*  
Amid the chaos, a band of souls so brave  
United in hope, their honor to save;  
They trod through lands where ruin reigned supreme,  
Each step a pledge, each heartbeat a dream.  
For in that dire hour, truth was harshly told,  
And destiny’s lament in sorrow’s script was scrolled.

*Chorus*  
O, sing the lament of war and fire,  
Of men and beasts in fierce, dark desire;  
Yet through the ashes, hope’s soft light is cast—  
For even shattered hearts may heal at last.

*Bridge*  
Beneath the silver gaze of a watchful moon,  
The battle raged in a sorrowful tune;  
Yet even as the night of despair drew near,  
A quiet promise stirred, both pure and clear.  
In the hearts of warriors, brave and true,  
Sprouted seeds of peace, born anew.

*Verse 4*  
At dawn, the tide of blood and sorrow ceased,  
The tyrants fell, and strife was released;  
In the silence after the storm’s fierce roar,  
Love and unity began to restore.  
Let this epic tale, with every scar, remind—  
Peace prevails when hopeful hearts are aligned.

*Final Chorus*  
O, sing the lament of war and fire,  
Yet let us cherish love that shall inspire;  
For in the ruins, from pain and despair,  
Rises the promise of peace beyond compare.  
In every tear and every fallen score,  
We find the light that guides us evermore.
